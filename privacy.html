DeuTech Fit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

DeuTech Fit ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that analyzes job postings on LinkedIn and StepStone against your CV. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • The developer of DeuTech Fit does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data. There is no DeuTech Fit server, account, analytics or advertising.
  • Your settings and CV text are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
  • When you click Analyze, the job posting and your CV text are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key.

Data the extension handles

To read the job you selected, the extension may also request that job's public posting page from linkedin.com. The request sends no cookies or personal data.

Google Gemini API

Analysis is performed by Google's Gemini API under your API key and your agreement with Google. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. In particular:

  • If you are in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK, Google applies its paid-service data terms even to free usage: your prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products.
  • Elsewhere, on the free (unpaid) tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. If you don't want that, use a paid Gemini API key, or remove sensitive details (phone number, address) from the CV text in Settings before analyzing.

Donations

The "Buy me a coffee" button opens buymeacoffee.com in a new tab. Donations are optional and handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee under its own privacy policy. The extension never sees or processes any payment information.

Data retention and deletion

All data stays in your browser until you remove it. To delete everything, either clear the fields in DeuTech Fit Settings and click Save, or uninstall the extension, which deletes all of its stored data.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data goes only to Google's Gemini API, as described above, to provide the extension's single purpose.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated and the new version published at this URL.
